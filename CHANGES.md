# Changes

## 2026-09-16 - third release

- Transcriptions reread against the digitisations of the register with the assistance of a language model and checked by the curator; every changed reading recorded in `transcription_corrections` with previous reading, folio and digitisation. Three responses absent from the July releases transcribed from the digitisations (currants regulation, folios 45r-48r; collegial opinion on the Narenta port, folios 28r-29r; silk duties of Treviso and Bassano, folio 6r); the response on the King of Sweden now begins on folio 6r. Dates corrected on the images (19 and 25 May 1609, 21 August 1552, 26 March 1610). Signatures, marginal notes and vote annotations read into their own fields.
- Qualified model of the opinions: 238 nuclei in 64 records, with glyphs, interactive graphs and English translation of every editorial text.
- Identifiers renumbered in the physical order of the register; July identifiers kept in `legacy_id`.
- Method page added; per-unit workflow badges removed from the reading interface.
- 64 units: 49 full transcriptions, 7 partial, 8 regests.

- Revision R142_0001-0062 (July numbering): 61 units; signatures separated from the diplomatic text; working notes moved to editorial notes; bilingual regests; two emendations logged; rule extractions regenerated with evidence.
